# LiteRT project governance

## Overview and Scope

LiteRT is a Google-led open-source project. This policy defines the technical
governance for [LiteRT](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/litert),
[litert-torch](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/litert-torch),
[LiteRT-LM](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/LiteRT-LM),
[litert-CLI](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/litert-CLI)and
[litert-samples](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/litert-samples).

Google retains final authority over the project's technical direction, roadmap,
release policies, and governance, and exercises this authority through its
designated project leads (@google-ai-edge/litert-leads). This governance model
does not establish community elections, binding votes, or support commitments,
and participation in any third-party ecosystem or landscape does not transfer
any intellectual property, trademark, or governance rights.

## Roles and Code Ownership

*   Contributors may submit issues, feedback, and pull requests in accordance
    with the Google Contributor License Agreement (CLA) and each repository's
    CONTRIBUTING.md and CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.
*   Maintainers’ are appointed by the project leads to review changes, triage
    issues, maintain code quality, and support releases for specific
    repositories or components.
*   Project Leads are appointed by Google to set cross-project technical
    direction, approve release and governance policies, appoint or remove
    maintainers, and resolve escalated technical questions.

Active maintainers, component owners, and any retired committers are documented
in each repository's .github/CODEOWNERS. Maintainer status and repository
permissions are granted explicitly by project leads, are not conferred
automatically by contribution volume or organizational affiliation, and may be
modified or revoked by project leads at any time (including upon changes in
role, activity, or employment).

## Decision-Making and Escalation

Day-to-day technical review and acceptance decisions are made by the responsible
repository or component maintainers. Proposals are evaluated at the maintainers'
discretion based on technical fit, maintainability, and project scope; approval
of a change does not guarantee inclusion in any specific release.

If a technical disagreement cannot be resolved with a maintainer on an issue or
pull request, a contributor or maintainer may request review by the project
leads in the relevant repository. Project leads make the final decision on
escalated matters.

## Releases, Policies, and Changes

Release scope, schedules, and compatibility policies are determined by the
project leads. Participation in the project is subject to the Apache 2.0
License, CONTRIBUTING.md, CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md, and SECURITY.md. Google retains
ownership of all LiteRT names, trademarks, and branding. This document does not
modify any license terms or create any obligation to accept contributions,
publish rationales, or maintain specific features or release schedules.

Updates to this document require approval by the project leads.
